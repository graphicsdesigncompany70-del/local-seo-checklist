## How Google decides local results

According to Google, local results are mainly influenced by:

| Factor | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Relevance | How well your profile matches the search |
| Distance | How close you are to the searcher |
| Prominence | How well-known and trusted your business appears |

[Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/) |

## Checklist

### 1. Google Business Profile

- [ ] Profile is claimed and verified
- [ ] Most specific primary category selected
- [ ] All services listed with short descriptions
- [ ] Opening hours are accurate (including holidays)
- [ ] Real photos of premises, team and work added
- [ ] Service areas added
- [ ] Website link opens the correct page
- [ ] Name, address and phone match your website exactly

### 2. Reviews

- [ ] A simple process exists to ask real customers for reviews
- [ ] Direct review link shared after good service
- [ ] Every review receives a reply, including negative ones
- [ ] No purchased or fake reviews

### 3. Website

- [ ] One page per core service
- [ ] Each service page mentions the area served
- [ ] Page loads quickly on mobile
- [ ] Phone number and enquiry form are easy to find
- [ ] Clear call to action on every key page
- [ ] FAQs answer real customer questions (cost, time, process)

### 4. Tracking

- [ ] Google Analytics 4 installed
- [ ] Google Search Console verified
- [ ] Calls, form submissions and WhatsApp clicks are tracked
- [ ] Current monthly enquiry numbers recorded as a baseline

### 5. Google Ads (if running paid campaigns)

- [ ] Conversion tracking tested
- [ ] Search terms reviewed regularly
- [ ] Negative keywords added
- [ ] Location targeting matches where customers actually are
- [ ] Landing page matches the ad's promise
- [ ] Ad spend tracked separately from management fees

## Metrics worth tracking

**SEO and local search:** organic enquiries, important search queries,
landing-page performance, Google Business Profile actions

**Paid campaigns:** leads, calls, form submissions, conversion rate,
cost per lead, ad spend

Tools: Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Google Tag Manager,
Google Ads, Google Business Profile, Looker Studio

## Common mistakes

1. Wrong or too-general Google Business Profile category
2. One generic "Services" page for everything
3. Measuring traffic instead of enquiries
4. Raising ad budget before fixing the landing page
5. Starting without any tracking

## Frequently asked questions

**What does digital marketing cost in Kolkata?**
Monthly management fees commonly start around ₹15,000 for small local
businesses, with ad spend separate. The scope decides the final figure.

**Should I start with SEO or Google Ads?**
SEO builds gradually. Google Ads can bring enquiries sooner but needs a good
landing page and tracking. Many businesses use both.

**Can anyone guarantee a number-one ranking?**
No. Be careful with anyone who promises it.
